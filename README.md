## 📱 Building Pocket Agents

### Your agents are working. You don't have to stay at your desk.

Pocket Agents brings your **Claude Code and Codex sessions to your phone**, so you can:

- 👀 Check progress across your computers
- 💬 Reply to questions and continue conversations
- 🔔 Know when your agents need you

I started building it because I wanted to step away from my desk without leaving my agents waiting. I use it in my own daily workflow and improve it around the problems I encounter.
